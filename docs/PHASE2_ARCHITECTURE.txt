PHASE 2 SAFETY CONTRACT
=======================

1. Phase 1 is the locked baseline. CRM, Admissions approval and Finance
   clearance retain their existing handlers and database functions.

2. Manual intake creates the same Admissions candidate/application shape with
   intake_route=manual and status=finance_pending. It does not create a student
   or bypass Admissions approval.

3. Finance clearance remains the only operation that provisions the Firestore
   student record. The provisioned identity starts disabled with lmsAccess=false.

4. Student lifecycle changes are server-only and organization-scoped. A client
   cannot activate LMS access with a direct dropdown write.

5. Active, Completed and Alumni require verified Finance clearance. Completed
   and Alumni retain LMS access so learning history, certificates and payment
   visibility are not accidentally lost.

6. Awaiting LMS Access, LMS Access Denied and Archived disable Firebase Auth and
   set lmsAccess=false.

7. "Delete Student" is a safe archive. It never deletes Finance, payment,
   Admissions or lifecycle audit history.

8. Firestore rules are intentionally unchanged. Rules hardening stays after
   the Admissions, Finance, student lifecycle, payment and analytics surfaces
   have all passed regression testing.

9. Phase 3 will add the canonical EMI/payment ledger and real-time student
   Payment/Learning Hub view without changing this lifecycle contract.
